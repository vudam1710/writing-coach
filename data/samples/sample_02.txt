Nowadays, celebrities are more famous for their glamour and wealth than for their achievements, and this has set a bad example for young children. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
---
Nowadays, some celebrities are better known for their glamour and wealth than for their real achievements. Some people believe that they are setting a bad example for young people. I partly agree with this view.
On the one hand, celebrities who often show their wealth can have a bad influence on young people. When they see these lifestyles on social media, they may think that they must be rich, successful and famous. As a result, they may only care about getting big results and forget that the process is also important. Moreover, more celebrities like this may give young people a one-sided view of success. They may think money and fame are the main goals, while education, skills and personal development are also important. They may also ignore about other important goals.
On the other hand, I do not completely agree that being rich makes celebrities bad role models. For some people, making a lot of money is their goal, and being wealthy can be a real achievement. If they earn money legally, there is nothing wrong with being proud of it or being admired for their success. Instead of worrying too much about rich celebrities, we should educate young people so that they can understand what is right and wrong. Also, the bad influence should come from the way celebrities use their money, such as spending it carelessly, rather than simply from having a lot of money. For example, celebrities can encourage young people working hard and use their success to help others.
In conclusion, I partly agree that showing wealth can set a bad example. However, being rich itself is not the problem; the way celebrities use their wealth is more important.
